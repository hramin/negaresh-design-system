## Scope

- Alerts, Banner, Toast, Badge/StatusBadge, Progress/Meter/UsageMeter, Spinner/GlobalLoadingBar, Skeleton, EmptyState, ErrorState/ErrorPage.
- Dialog/AlertDialog (bottom sheet on mobile), Sheet, Popover, Tooltip, ContextualHelp.
- SideNav → TabStrip, Tabs, Breadcrumbs, Pagination/LoadMore, Stepper, Disclosure/Accordion, UserMenu (composed on `AccountPill` + `Menu`), Footer; `Sheet`-backed mobile drawer integration for `TopBar`. *(Note: `TopBar`, `TopBarBrand`, `TopBarNav`, `TopBarNavLink`, and `AccountPill` were pulled forward and delivered in the P2 follow-up.)*
- DataTable (sort, select, stacked mobile), FilterBar, BulkActionBar, KeyValueList, StatTile/StatGroup, Timeline/ActivityList, DiffView, Avatar/AvatarGroup, VerifiedBadge, Card, List/GridList, Image.
